How It Works: PolliNode & Data Path

From field signals to measurable grower decisions.
1. Sense

Multimodal Edge Sensing: Digital MEMS Microphone, VOC, Temp/Humidity, Light Sensors.

2. Edge QC

Raspberry Pi Compute Module 4 filters local noise and verifies sensor integrity.

3. Predict

Crop-specific models forecast pollination risk window over 14-day / 72-hour horizons.

4. Action

Actionable alerts sent to growers via app to optimize hive placement and interventions.
